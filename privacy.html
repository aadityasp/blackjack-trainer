Privacy Policy for Blackjack Trainer

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Blackjack Trainer ("the app") is a free blackjack basic-strategy practice trainer. This policy explains what data the app collects. The short version: none.

Summary

  • The app does not collect any personal data.
  • The app does not require an account, sign-up, or login of any kind.
  • The app works completely offline and does not send any data anywhere.
  • All of your progress, statistics, and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics tools, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What data we collect

None. The app has no servers, no backend, and makes no network requests during normal use. We (the developer) never receive, see, or have access to anything you do in the app.

What data is stored on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the app stores the following locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage:

  • Your chosen table-rule settings (deck count, dealer soft-17 rule, payout, surrender, double-after-split, etc.)
  • Your practice statistics: accuracy, streaks, and per-hand mastery data
  • App preferences such as sound, haptics, motion, and contrast toggles

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us, to the app stores, or to any third party. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Children's privacy

Blackjack Trainer is a practice/education tool for adults and is rated for mature audiences due to its simulated-gambling theme (no real money, wagering, or payouts are involved anywhere in the app). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, because the app does not collect information at all.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with any third party because no data is collected in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds an optional online feature), we will update this page and revise the effective date above. As of the effective date shown, this policy accurately describes the app in its entirety.
