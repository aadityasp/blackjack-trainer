What it is

Blackjack Trainer is a free practice app that teaches basic strategy, the play that gives the house the smallest possible edge. You play real hands against a dealer and get instant feedback the moment your move differs from the correct one.

  • No real money, no wagering, no payouts. It is a practice tool, not a gambling app.
  • No account, no signup, no ads.
  • Works fully offline. Your progress stays on your device.

Basic strategy charts

Not sure of the correct play for a hand? The free blackjack basic strategy charts show it for every hand, for dealers that hit or stand on soft 17, with the reason behind each play.
