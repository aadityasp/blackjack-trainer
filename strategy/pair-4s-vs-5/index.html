Pair of 4s vs dealer 5: hit, stand, or split?

Split (hit if double after split is not allowed). Pair of 4s against a dealer 5 is played the same way whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17.

  • Dealer hits soft 17SplitIf double after split is not allowed: hit
  • Dealer stands on soft 17SplitIf double after split is not allowed: hit
  • Hit: take one more card.
  • Split: separate a pair into two hands and play each one on its own.

Why

Kept together, a pair of 4s is a weak hand. Split apart, each 4 starts its own hand, and against a dealer 5 two hands do better than one. This split relies on double after split being allowed, which these charts assume. If it is not allowed, hit instead.

A pair is two cards of the same value, and any two 10-value cards count as a pair of 10s. Played as one hand, this pair is a hard 8: see Hard 8 vs dealer 5.

Rules assumed

  • 4, 6, or 8 decks (the chart is the same for all three)
  • The dealer checks for blackjack when showing a 10 or an ace, before you act
  • Double down is allowed on any first two cards
  • Double after split is allowed
  • Late surrender is offered, as your first decision only
  • Split up to four hands; split aces get one card each and cannot be resplit

One-deck and two-deck games, tables without surrender, and dealers who do not check for blackjack change some plays. Check your table's rules before relying on any chart.

Common questions: Pair of 4s vs dealer 5

What is the correct play for a pair of 4s against a dealer 5?

Split (hit if double after split is not allowed). This is basic strategy for 4 to 8 decks, and it is the same whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17.

Does the soft 17 rule change a pair of 4s against a dealer 5?

No. The play is the same whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17. Only a handful of hands on the chart change with that rule, and this is not one of them.

Should I split 4s against a dealer 5?

Yes. Kept together, a pair of 4s is a weak hand. Split apart, each 4 starts its own hand, and against a dealer 5 two hands do better than one. This split relies on double after split being allowed, which these charts assume. If it is not allowed, hit instead.

Does basic strategy guarantee a win?

No. It picks the play with the best average result, but any single hand can go either way, and the house keeps a small edge. This site and the Blackjack Trainer app are for practice only.
