Blackjack basic strategy chart: the dealer stands on soft 17 (4 to 8 decks)

The correct play for every hand against every dealer upcard, for tables where the dealer stands on soft 17. Tap or click any cell for the reason and the numbers behind it.

Playing where the dealer hits soft 17? Use the dealer hits soft 17 chart instead.

Rules this chart assumes

  • 4, 6, or 8 decks (the chart is the same for all three)
  • The dealer checks for blackjack when showing a 10 or an ace, before you act
  • Double down is allowed on any first two cards
  • Double after split is allowed
  • Late surrender is offered, as your first decision only
  • Split up to four hands; split aces get one card each and cannot be resplit

One-deck and two-deck games, tables without surrender, and dealers who do not check for blackjack change some plays. Check your table's rules before relying on any chart.

Chart key

  • DsDouble down, or stand if you cannot
  • PSplit

What changes with the soft 17 rule

6 of the 360 hands play differently between the two rules. These are the 6:

  • Hard 11 vs dealer ace: hit when the dealer stands on soft 17, double down (hit if you cannot double) when the dealer hits soft 17.
  • Hard 15 vs dealer ace: hit when the dealer stands on soft 17, surrender (hit if surrender is not offered) when the dealer hits soft 17.
  • Hard 17 vs dealer ace: stand when the dealer stands on soft 17, surrender (stand if surrender is not offered) when the dealer hits soft 17.
  • Soft 18 vs dealer 2: stand when the dealer stands on soft 17, double down (stand if you cannot double) when the dealer hits soft 17.
  • Soft 19 vs dealer 6: stand when the dealer stands on soft 17, double down (stand if you cannot double) when the dealer hits soft 17.
  • Pair of 8s vs dealer ace: split when the dealer stands on soft 17, surrender (split if surrender is not offered) when the dealer hits soft 17.

Questions about this chart

Should I use the chart for a dealer who hits or stands on soft 17?

Use the one that matches the table you are playing. The rule is usually printed on the table or the rules card, as "dealer hits soft 17" or "dealer stands on all 17s". Only 6 of the 360 hands on the chart play differently between the two.

Is the chart different for 4, 6, and 8 decks?

No. The chart is the same for 4, 6, and 8 decks. One-deck and two-deck games change a few plays, so check the number of decks too.

What do the letters on the dealer stands on soft 17 chart mean?

H is hit and S is stand. D is double down, or hit if you cannot, and Ds is double down, or stand if you cannot. P is split, and Ph is split if double after split is allowed, otherwise hit. Rh, Rs, and Rp are surrender, or hit, stand, or split if surrender is not offered.

Does basic strategy guarantee a win?

No. Basic strategy picks the play with the best average result for every hand, but any single hand can go either way, and the house keeps a small edge. This site and the Blackjack Trainer app are for practice only.
